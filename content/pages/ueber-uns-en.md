        <h2>Why TrailnError?</h2>

        <p>
            TrailnError isn't about every tour going perfectly.
            What motivates us is trying new things and taking on challenges
            that might be a little beyond what we've done before.
            Good planning is just as much a part of that as being able
            to rethink things along the way.
        </p>

        <p>
            Mistakes, uncertainty and changes of plan are part of being in the mountains.
            The weather doesn't cooperate, equipment causes problems or
            you're simply not feeling your best that day.
            That's when the plan changes – or when it's time to turn around.
        </p>

        <p>
            Those are exactly the moments we don't want to leave out of our tour reports.
            If something didn't work, we had to change our plans or a tour turned out
            completely differently than expected, that's just as much a part of the story
            as reaching the summit or the finish.
        </p>

        <p class="about-why__statement">
            <strong>Error is part of the game.</strong>
        </p>

    </div>
